Who I Am

Engineer. Builder. Problem Solver.

I'm a graduate Electrical Engineer from the University of British Columbia, with a Biomedical Engineering concentration. My academic journey has spanned circuit design, embedded systems, MEMS fabrication, PLC automation, control systems, and biomedical instrumentation.

Based in Vernon, BC, I've worked on everything from reverse engineering industrial PCBs under manufacturing constraints, to designing soft robotic actuators powered by hydraulic pressure, to studying MEMS chevron actuators fabricated with an in-house SU-8 process. Each project has pushed me to integrate engineering principles across multiple disciplines.

My coursework spans two distinct tracks: a Biomedical Engineering concentration covering bioinstrumentation, MEMS, and medical device design, alongside deep hands-on experience in industrial automation through PLC programming, HMI development, and electropneumatic systems via FESTO certification. I'm equally drawn to both fields and genuinely open to pursuing a career in either — or wherever the two overlap.
